This page exercises the right-hand collapsible TOC. Every heading level from h1 to h6 appears below; h1/h2 sections start expanded, deeper levels start collapsed. Clicking an entry scrolls to the section, and the section currently in view is highlighted as you scroll. Below 1100px viewport width the TOC hides entirely.
